TESSELLATE Sky Survey

Source catalogs from an untargeted search of every TESS full-frame image

TESS tiles the sky with four cameras, sector after sector, watching each field continuously for about a month. The TESSELLATE Sky Survey (TSS) runs the TESSELLATE pipeline over the difference images of every full-frame image, searching for anything that changes with no target list, and turns what it finds into public catalogs: asteroids crossing the field, flares on nearby stars and, in preparation, variable stars, transients and exoplanets.

Pipeline

Every catalog starts from the same reduction, so the photometry, detections and quality flags are consistent across them.

TESS full-frame imagesEvery camera, every sector, from the TESS archive.
TESSreduceBackground subtraction, alignment and difference imaging (Ridden-Harper et al. 2021).
TESSELLATEUntargeted detection and classification of every change in the difference images (Roxburgh et al. 2025).
CatalogsScience-specific measurement and vetting for each source class, published as searchable sites.
